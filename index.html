01About

I’m a Computer Science student at UT Dallas who moved from Karachi to Dallas to build a career on my own terms. I think ahead: internships, technical skills and financial independence were on my mind from the first year, and I’d rather shape my path than let circumstances decide it.

Leadership comes naturally. I’ve run logistics for Whales Comic Con, drawing 550+ attendees two years in a row, and helped organise events like Whales Carnival and AnimeCon. I’m comfortable owning the people, the plan and the outcome.

I’m analytical and a healthy skeptic. I question answers that don’t add up, which is exactly what engineering and research demand. I’m also resourceful and happy to start at the bottom, taking campus jobs and learning programming fundamentals properly instead of pretending to be an expert.

I’m honest about my growth edges: I tend to over-optimise the future, and I’m working on patience and consistent execution. My goal is to become a strong technical leader, and one day, a founder.
